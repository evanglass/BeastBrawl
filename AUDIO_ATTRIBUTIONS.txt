PROJECT AUDIO REVIEW NOTES

The list above covers audio assigned to the 24 character sound slots in Assets/Resources/CharacterSfxManager.prefab (some slots share a source). License checks reflect the source listings available on 2026-11-15. Attribution for CC0 sources is included as a courtesy.

Still to verify before release: Freesound 507730 (keskaowl), 77637 (lendrick), and 241562 (reitanna). Their source pages were not accessible during this review; credit alone does not establish permission. Piddipop's 728102 is listed as CC0 by its uploader but is described as sampling an orc recording by cylon8472; the original sample's rights chain needs verification.

Unassigned candidate sounds and source versions have been archived outside Unity Assets in Archived Sound Effects/FeralFightSoundFX and are not part of Unity builds. Review licenses if any are restored or distributed. One archived file named ' for .wav' has no identified source.

Music files in Assets/MusicSystem/Resources/Music have no author, source, or license documentation located in this review. Confirm ownership or permission and any sample/loop licenses with their creator before public release.
